Up next shows the next three events from any calendar on your Google account's secret iCal link, with a relative "in 46 min" on the first. All-day events show as "All day". It is read-only; Select on the card could open the Google Calendar app if installed. To do lists open Todoist tasks ordered by due date, with overdue first. Moving to a task and pressing Select ticks it: the row shows a check and dims for a few seconds, then drops off the list, and Todoist marks it complete. Both blocks refresh every few minutes and when the TV wakes. If a block has no connection yet it shows a single line, "Connect in Settings", instead of empty space.
